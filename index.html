Merge Puzzle

Game Description

Enter a mystical world where dragons rule, and merging is the key to unlocking their power! Tales & Dragons: Merge Puzzle is a thrilling puzzle game where you combine magical items, hatch legendary dragons, and discover hidden secrets of an enchanted realm. Merge wisely, strategize your growth, and become the ultimate dragon master!

How to Play

Merge to Evolve
Combine three or more identical items to create powerful new ones
Hatch & Grow Dragons
Collect dragon eggs, nurture them, and evolve them into mighty creatures
Expand Your Kingdom
Unlock new lands, discover hidden treasures, and restore the magical world
Strategize Your Moves
Every merge matters! Plan your strategy to maximize rewards
Complete Magical Quests
Solve puzzles and complete missions to uncover legendary artifacts

Color in Game Design

Did you know that blue shades improve concentration while yellow stimulates creativity? That’s why Tales & Dragons uses a mix of these colors to enhance player experience. Fun fact: The original design was entirely purple, but testers said it made them too relaxed to merge!
